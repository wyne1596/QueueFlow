
## Work status
- Not Started: the request is recorded but work has not begun.
- In Progress: the business is preparing or performing the service.
- Ready: the product or service is finished and ready for pickup or collection.
- Received: the customer has received the product or service.
- Cancelled: the request was cancelled.

## Arrival / queue status
- Not Arrived: the customer has not arrived at the shop.
- Waiting: the customer has arrived and joined the queue.
- Being Served: the customer is currently being attended to.
- Departed: the customer has left or has been skipped.

Only Waiting and Being Served customers appear in the active queue. Scheduled orders can move through Not Started, In Progress,
and Ready while remaining Not Arrived. When the customer arrives, the owner changes the arrival status to Waiting.
When service or collection is finished, the owner can mark the customer as Received.

Call and SMS reminder controls show simulated previews only. The prototype does not place real calls or send real messages.
